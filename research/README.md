# 📚 Research Directory: Knowledge Base, 240 Papers & Findings

Welcome to the **Research** directory. This is the intellectual center of the repository, containing the **240-paper literature corpus**, the **Master Knowledge Base**, 1-page dossiers, and synthesis findings.

---

## 📂 Folder Structure

```
research/
├── knowledge_base/                    # Master Knowledge Base & Oral Defense Guide
│   ├── README.md                      # Knowledge Base portal & executive overview
│   ├── paradigms_and_solutions_matrix.md # 10-paradigm comparison matrix & trade-offs
│   ├── failure_modes_and_solutions_qa.md # 7 critical VLM failure modes (root causes, math, solutions)
│   ├── interactive_qa_guide.md        # 25 study, defense & examination questions & answers
│   └── one_page_dossiers/             # 10 standalone 1-page track dossiers (ASCII pipelines, Q&A)
│
├── reviews/                           # 240 granular, 1-by-1 structured paper reviews
│   ├── categories/                    # 10 categorized subfolders (24 reviews per folder)
│   └── indices/                       # Master indices (by relevance, category, catalog)
│
├── papers/                            # Bibliography & extracted paper summaries
│   ├── bibliography.bib               # Central 240-entry BibTeX citation database
│   └── extracted/                     # 240 plain-text extractions
│
├── findings/                          # Cross-paper syntheses, metrics & logs
│   ├── comparative_matrix.md          # Benchmark comparisons (Direct, Selection, Oracle, SOTA)
│   ├── research_log.md                # Running chronological research ledger
│   ├── taxonomy.md                    # 4-dimensional architectural taxonomy
│   ├── synthesis/                     # 4 deep strategic synthesis reports
│   └── roadmaps/                      # Concrete engineering implementation roadmaps
│
└── docs/                              # Project ground truth & formal specifications
    ├── problem_statement.md           # XLRS-Bench findings, crossover effect, and research questions
    └── specs/                         # XLRS-Bench evaluation split specifications
```

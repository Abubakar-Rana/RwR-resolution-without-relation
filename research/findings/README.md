# Research Findings & Cross-Paper Synthesis (`findings/`)

This directory is the high-level synthesis engine of the repository. While [`reviews/notes/`](file:///d:/projects/research/vlm/reviews/notes/) dissects papers individually, `findings/` integrates discoveries across papers to discover trends, trade-offs, and competitive benchmarks.

---

## Directory Contents

- [`comparative_matrix.md`](file:///d:/projects/research/vlm/findings/comparative_matrix.md): Master side-by-side comparison table of all reviewed models, architectures, token efficiency, and benchmark scores.
- [`taxonomy.md`](file:///d:/projects/research/vlm/findings/taxonomy.md): Categorical classification of VLM architectural paradigms (projectors, resolution strategies, training paradigms, compression techniques).
- [`research_log.md`](file:///d:/projects/research/vlm/findings/research_log.md): Continuous, chronological collaboration ledger tracking daily research progress, agent handoffs, hypotheses tested, and strategic pivots.

---

## Instructions for AI Agents

1. **Keep the Matrix Synchronized**: Whenever a new paper review is added to `reviews/notes/`, add a corresponding row in [`comparative_matrix.md`](file:///d:/projects/research/vlm/findings/comparative_matrix.md).
2. **Log Every Handoff**: Every agent finishing a session or major research unit must append an entry to [`research_log.md`](file:///d:/projects/research/vlm/findings/research_log.md).

# SkySense: A Multi-Modal Remote Sensing Foundation Model Towards Universal Geospatial Understanding

- **Authors**: Zichen Guo, Lin Lan, Mengxi Chen, Fangrun Wang, et al.
- **Year**: 2024
- **Venue**: arXiv preprint
- **arXiv**: 2312.10115
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10115
- **BibTeX Key**: `guo2024skysense`

## Abstract
Building universal foundation models for Earth Observation requires accommodating diverse sensor modalities, variable temporal resolutions, and vast spatial scales. We present SkySense, a multi-modal foundation model featuring 2.06 billion parameters trained on over 21 million multi-source satellite images. SkySense employs a temporal-spatial cross-attention architecture to integrate multi-granularity observations, achieving state-of-the-art results across 16 downstream remote sensing benchmarks including scene classification, object detection, and semantic segmentation.

## Key Extracted Contributions
1. Multi-granularity spatial representation learning enables consistent feature extraction from local patches to broad landscapes.
2. Scale-aware attention reduces representation collapse on small aerial objects.
